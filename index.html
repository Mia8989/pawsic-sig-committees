Step 2

Invite and Onboard Your Members

You invite the members who have reached out to you directly. Use this sample email to bring in co-chairs and initial members and point them to the signing forms.

Subject: You're invited to join the PAWSIC [Committee Name] Shared Interest Group

Dear [Member Name],

We're excited to invite you to join the PAWSIC [Committee Name] Shared Interest Group (SIG). This committee brings together [brief description of focus] to advance [committee mission].

Before our first meeting on [Date], please complete the following:

  1. Review and sign the Conflict of Interest (COI) form: [COI Form Link]
  2. Review and sign the Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA): [NDA Form Link]

Please complete both by [Deadline Date] so we can move forward. These documents help us maintain transparency and protect our committee's confidential discussions.

Our first meeting is on [Date] at [Time] via Zoom. You'll receive a calendar invite with the link once members have signed.

Warm regards,
[Your Name]
PAWSIC [Committee Name] Chair

Tip: Personalize the committee name, dates, and links before sending. Admin can help you build onboarding forms that also collect member resumes and availability.

Watch: ROOT activation walkthrough

Expand below for details on the verification screen, privacy, and managing access.

Why do I see a warning screen at first?▾

On your first visit to root.pawsic.org you may see "Google hasn't verified this app." Because the portal is built as a hybrid tool that external partners can also use (for example @gmail.com or @aol.com), Google shows a maximum caution alert until we finish official registration.

To continue safely: click "Advanced," then "Go to ROOT [EXT] (unsafe)." The wording is Google's standard label for apps still in review.

What can this app access?▾

The app requests only your email address, purely to know who is logging in and load your dashboard.

It cannot read your emails, access your Google Drive files, or manage your Google account. It simply connects to our database to fetch your portal data.

Is the app being verified by Google?▾

Yes. We're moving the app through Google's official OAuth verification. Until that finishes, you can safely bypass the warning as described above. Once approved, the warning disappears and external users see only a standard prompt confirming their email.

How do I revoke access?▾

You can remove access anytime:

  1. Go to your Google Account settings.
  2. Click Data & Privacy.
  3. Under Data from apps and services you use, select Third-party apps & services.
  4. Select our app and choose Remove Access.
What files will I see?▾

You'll see only the Drive resources you have access to as a viewer or editor, plus anything shared as viewable to anyone with the link. If you don't have access to anything yet, you'll be sent to a short form to request it.

Trouble signing in? Use the exact assigned email, clear your browser cookies if you were logged in before, and contact PAWSIC support if it persists.

Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add members after the first meeting?

Yes. Invite additional members anytime. They complete the COI and NDA, then join from the next meeting onward.

Who has access to the committee Google Drive?

Typically the Chair and Co-Chair have full access. Individual members may be added to specific folders as your committee needs.

When do we upload bylaws and policy?

After your first meeting, once the group has reviewed and finalized them, upload to your Drive folder.

How do I reschedule a booked meeting?

Use the scheduler again for a new time, or contact the PAWSIC administrator to cancel and rebook.

Who do I contact with questions?

Reach the PAWSIC program administrator at dahlia@pawsic.org.
