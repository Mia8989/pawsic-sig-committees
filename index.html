Schedule Committee Meetings

Learn how to book meetings and add your committee members using the PAWSIC Zoom scheduler.

How to Use the Scheduler

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Select your date and time: Pick a time that works for your committee.
  2. Add member emails: In the "Additional Attendees" field, paste your committee members' email addresses separated by commas. For example: jane@example.com, john@example.com, sarah@example.com
  3. Complete the booking: The meeting link will be automatically sent to you and all attendees via email.

Prepare Governance Documents

Use these templates to prepare your committee's required governance documents. Each template is provided as a view-only Google Doc that you can copy and customize for your specific committee.

Conflict of Interest (COI) & Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

Committee members must complete both documents before attending their first meeting. Follow this workflow:

1

Access Templates

Click the buttons below to view the COI and NDA templates. Each opens as a view-only Google Doc.

View COI Template →
View NDA Template →
2

Copy & Customize

Make a copy of each template (File > Make a copy). Customize the text for your specific committee's needs. You do not need approval from PAWSIC—make the documents work for your group.

3

Share with Admin

Once your finalized documents are ready, share them with the PAWSIC program administrator. Email your customized COI and NDA documents along with the list of committee members who need to sign.

4

Admin Creates JotForm

PAWSIC will create a JotForm with your finalized document text. This form will include signature fields and will be set up for your committee members to sign digitally.

5

Distribute Signing Form

Once the JotForm is ready, PAWSIC will send it to you to distribute to your committee members. Share the signing link with your group and collect completed forms before the first meeting.

Access Your Committee's Google Drive

Your committee's shared Google Drive is the central hub for all documents, meeting recordings, agendas, and collaborative work.

About Your Committee Drive

Your designated Google Drive folder contains:

  • Meeting agendas and minutes
  • Meeting recordings and transcripts
  • Committee governance documents (bylaws, policies)
  • Member contact information
  • Shared resources and templates

Root.pawsic.org Access

Login Instructions

  1. Use Your Designated Email: Log in using the specific email address provided to you by the PAWSIC administrator. This email is linked to your committee's access.
  2. Visit root.pawsic.org: Go to the portal in your web browser.
  3. Enter Your Email: When prompted, enter your assigned email address.
  4. Authenticate: Follow the authentication steps to verify your identity.
  5. Access Your Drive: Once logged in, you'll see your committee's shared drive and all linked resources.

Troubleshooting Login Issues

Having trouble accessing? Make sure you:

  • Are using the exact email address assigned to you by the PAWSIC administrator
  • Have cleared your browser cookies if you were logged in previously
  • Contact PAWSIC support if you continue to have issues

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add members after the first meeting?

Yes. You can invite additional members at any time. They will need to complete the COI and NDA forms and then attend from the next scheduled meeting onward.

Who has access to the committee's Google Drive?

Typically, the Chair and Co-Chair have full access to the Google Drive. Individual members may be added to specific shared folders or files as appropriate for your committee's structure.

When should we upload bylaws and policy documents?

These should be uploaded to your committee's Google Drive folder after your first committee meeting, once the group has reviewed and finalized them.

How do I reschedule a meeting I've already booked?

Use the Zoom scheduler again to book a different time, or contact the PAWSIC program administrator if you need to cancel an existing meeting and reschedule.

What if I need help creating my committee's bylaws and policy?

The PAWSIC program administrator can provide guidance and the example documents to help you get started. We don't create these for you, but we're happy to review your drafts and offer suggestions.

Who do I contact if I have questions?

Reach out to the PAWSIC program administrator at dahlia@pawsic.org with any questions or concerns.
